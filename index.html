I’m an independent software developer based in Skopje, North Macedonia. Mobile development is my main focus, alongside robotics and building websites for clients.

I study at the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering (FINKI, UKIM), and work with people to turn their ideas into thoughtful, functional software.
